# BL-002 - Pixel Surface Studio

**Payoff:** separate surface detail from broad authored shade by editing a small atlas, UV regions, and vertex-color contribution.

**Mechanism:** original image texture with Closest sampling, explicit UVs, a mesh color attribute, and a material graph combining texture/color information. The graph is editable in Shader Editor.

**Try:** inspect the image at native resolution, compare nearby atlas regions, inspect face-corner colors, and change Experiment value and apply it. Use a saved copy to compare texture-only and color-modulated appearance.

**Reset:** reset regenerates the original owned fixture. Preserve painted variations in a separate file/image first.

**Automated gate:** verify UV data, color attribute, image interpolation, and material node wiring. **Editor gate:** inspect the graph, UV editor, and color data. **Visual gate:** keep the atlas readable and shade shapes coherent under the recorded view transform.

**Limit:** nearest sampling does not author pixel art; color management and interpolation alter rendered colors. Export preservation of a color attribute needs its own receiving-tool gate. Technique lineage comes from supplied ABYSS X ZERO research, with original atlas content.
