# BL-004 - Motion Signal

**Payoff:** make a simple original mechanism move, then change its rhythm by editing keys.

**Mechanism:** editable animation data and keyframed transforms evaluated on the timeline. The experiment separates authored keys from Blender's interpolated motion.

**Try:** inspect the starting and ending pose, scrub intermediate frames, then compare interpolation in a saved copy. Change Experiment value and apply it; check whether both motion amplitude and silhouette remain clear.

**Reset:** reset restores the owned animation and frame range.

**Automated gate:** evaluate time-separated transforms and assert motion rather than merely the presence of an action. **Editor gate:** inspect keys and timeline, play/scrub, save/reopen. **Visual gate:** motion remains understandable at the representative camera distance.

**Limit:** this is keyframe animation, not a production rig, retargeting solution, or universal stepped-animation policy. Universal on-2s timing was not established by the supplied visual research.
