## Evidence vocabulary

**Confirmed** is directly established by an inspected source or executed check. **Observed** describes visible output without proving hidden implementation. **Recommended** is an original lab/production choice. **Unknown** is unresolved. Preserve these distinctions when adding numerical budgets or recreating a technique.

## No game asset dependency

Public workshop names, palette, meshes, atlas motifs, and scene layouts are original. Research lineage explains decisions without packaging another game's art or asserting equivalence to its production pipeline.
